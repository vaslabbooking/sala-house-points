House mascots
=============

One image per house, named after the house in lower case:

    bears        eagles        sharks        tigers

The extension can be any of: svg, png, webp, jpg, jpeg, gif. They are tried in
that order, so bears.svg wins over bears.png if both exist. Delete the one you
are replacing to avoid confusion.

To use student-designed crests, save each drawing over the matching name --
bears.png, eagles.png and so on -- and redeploy. Nothing else needs changing. A
house with no usable image is simply skipped, so you can swap them one at a
time.

Two things to watch if you are scanning or photographing artwork:

  * Use a TRANSPARENT background (PNG or WebP). The leaderboard is very dark,
    so a scan on white paper shows up as a white box around the crest.

  * Make it at least 1000px on the long side. The mascot is scaled up as it
    bursts towards the viewer, and a small image goes soft. Vector (SVG) stays
    sharp at any size, which is why it is tried first.

The four supplied files are plain flat-vector heads drawn to a common style.
They are placeholders -- replacing them is expected.
