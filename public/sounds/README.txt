Mascot sounds
=============

Drop one audio file per house here, named after the house in lower case:

    bears.mp3
    eagles.mp3
    sharks.mp3
    tigers.mp3

The matching sound plays once as the winning house's mascot bursts onto the
screen. A missing file is ignored silently, so it is fine to add them one at a
time, or to add none at all.

Keep them short — roughly one to two seconds. Anything longer runs past the
animation and over whoever is speaking.

Turn playback on in Admin -> Settings -> Leaderboard ("Mascot sound"). It is
off by default.

Where to find sounds that are free to use:
  - freesound.org  (filter the licence to "Creative Commons 0")
  - pixabay.com/sound-effects  (free for commercial use, no attribution)
  - zapsplat.com  (free with an account; attribution required on the free tier)

Check the licence on anything you download and keep a note of it.
